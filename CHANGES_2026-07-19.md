# Addendum 2 — Entry semantics defect found and corrected

Cross-referencing `candidates_tier1_full.csv` against the source OHLC (trader-initiated) confirmed
the price data was exact, but exposed a defect in the entry rule.

- **The bug:** v1.3 entered when price was *beyond* the threshold during the window, rather than
  when it *crossed* the threshold. 163 of 291 candidates had already breached before 15:40 and
  fired automatically at window open (108 at exactly 15:40).
- **It also invalidated a documented conclusion.** v1.3 §9 closed RF-004's prior-touch half by
  citing T1-008's "first in-window crossing at 15:40". No such crossing exists — price crossed at
  15:35 and stayed below. The right answer had been produced by the wrong mechanism.
- **The fix (Rulebook v1.4 §2.4/§2.5):** crossing semantics, a 5-minute pre-window grace with
  entry clamped to window open, and a 1.5 h origin age bound. Candidates 291 -> **111 (1.0/day)**.
  6 of 7 known entries reproduced with exact zone bounds and times; the 7th (T1-009) is produced
  with correct bounds but shifted 16:00 -> 15:40 by the 0.3 pip tolerance, independently
  confirming the RF-002 side effect.
- **RF-004 now closes as parameterised:** prior touch is "preserve briefly" (5 min). Activation
  rules B and C remain indistinguishable on all twelve examples.

Four parameters now ship UNVALIDATED: tolerance 0.3 pips, grace 5 min, origin age 1.5 h, sweep
lookback W=2. All four are fitted to twelve examples. RF-003 is the only fully open rule flag.

Rulebook v1.3 was deleted; v1.0 and v1.4 remain.
